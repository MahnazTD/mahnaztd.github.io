I’ve always been fascinated by how language can *mix ideas from different worlds* —  
especially where science and creativity meet.  
This page gathers a few of the **hybrid linguistic concepts** I have coined and explored in my research.

---

### 🌱 Eco-Metaphor
I introduced the term **Ecometaphor** to describe the connection between ecology and emotion in language.  
It explores how environmental factors influence the way people express emotions—especially *anger*—across cultures.  
This idea was presented in my 2015 paper, *“Ecometaphor: The Effect of Ecology and Environment on Shaping Anger Metaphors in Different Cultures,”*  
published in *Language & Ecology* (International Ecolinguistics Association).

---

### 🧠 Ergonolinguistics
I coined **Ergonolinguistics** to link **language, semiotics, and ergonomics**.  
This concept studies how cultural and linguistic symbols affect product design, visual communication, and accessibility.  
It emphasizes user-friendly and inclusive communication, including Braille and ergonomic visual design.  
My 2019 paper on this topic appeared in the *International Journal of Language & Applied Linguistics.*

---

### 🌍 Eco-Dialectometry
I developed the concept of **Eco-Dialectometry**, integrating **ecological, cultural, and environmental variables**  
into dialectometric research.  
It examines how climate, vegetation, and altitude shape lexical variation across regions.  
I am currently writing a paper on this topic.
