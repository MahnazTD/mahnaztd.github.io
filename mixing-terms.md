I’ve always loved how language can *mix ideas from different worlds* — 
especially when technical fields meet creativity.  
This page collects the funny, strange, and beautiful hybrid terms I invent or notice.

---

### 🌱 Eco-Metaphor
A way of seeing metaphors as *living ecosystems*:  
they grow, adapt, compete, and sometimes die — just like species in an environment.

---

### 🌍 Eco-Dialectometry
Integrating **ecological, cultural, and environmental variables** 
into dialectometric studies.  
For example, how climate, vegetation, and altitude influence lexical variation.

---

### 🧠 Ergonolinguistics
Language meets ergonomics — how *comfort, design, and efficiency* 
affect language use and documentation workspaces.  
A linguistic researcher’s desk says a lot about their cognitive “grammar”.

---

### 🔬 Bio-Semantics
The study of *semantic life cycles* — how meanings are born, mutate, and go extinct.  
A metaphorical borrowing from biology, reminding us that language evolves like DNA.

---

### 💡 Why These Matter
I like to think of these as **“linguistic thought experiments”** —  
they start as jokes or creative sparks, but they can grow into real research directions.  
Each one carries both humor and depth, a reminder that science and creativity can coexist beautifully.
