## 🌍 The Future of Linguistics

The future is always unknown — a landscape wrapped in mystery.  
Sometimes we imagine it, perhaps to quiet our minds or give shape to what we cannot control.

Lately, I’ve found myself thinking a lot about the future of linguistics — especially under the growing shadow of Artificial Intelligence and the rapid transformations shaping our world.

In my field, the study of endangered languages, we witness how smaller languages slowly fade, their speakers shifting toward a few dominant tongues.  
It makes me wonder: can AI become a guardian of linguistic diversity, or will it hasten the loss of voices that already struggle to be heard?

I don’t know the answer.  
Maybe none of us do.  
However, that uncertainty is what makes this conversation worthwhile.

✨ Let’s explore together:  
**What do you think about the future of linguistics?**  
In your own field, what might happen?
