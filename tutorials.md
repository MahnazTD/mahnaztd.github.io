## Lexique Pro Tutorial

This tutorial introduces Lexique Pro as a practical tool for creating and publishing digital dictionaries. It is designed for users with little or no technical background who wish to develop lexical resources for under-documented or community languages. 

The tutorial guides users through the process of preparing lexical data, importing it into Lexique Pro, and generating a searchable HTML dictionary that can be shared online. By following these steps, users can transform simple word lists into structured, accessible, and user-friendly dictionary interfaces.
